Privacy Policy

Draft for v1 – have it reviewed before public release. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Game data: your driver name, garage designs, coins, purchases, race results, leaderboard entries and weekly series progress.
  • On your device only: settings (language, volume, graphics quality) and an offline cache of your garage.

How we use it

To run the game: saving your progress, showing leaderboards (only your driver name and scores are shown to other players), and validating purchases.

Ads and payments

v1 contains placeholder ads and payments only. No ad network or payment provider receives your data in v1. This policy will be updated before real ads or payments are enabled.

Shared sign-in

Picco Garage currently uses the same sign-in service as another app by the same developer. Deleting your game data removes all Picco Garage data; your sign-in account itself is kept.

Your choices

You can change your driver name, log out, or delete all your game data at any time in Settings → Account.

Children

Picco Garage is a non-violent driving game suitable for general audiences. Players under the age required by local law need a parent or guardian’s permission to sign in.

Contact

Questions: contact the developer through the app store listing or project page.
