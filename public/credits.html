Sound & music credits

All sound effects in Picco Garage v1 are original: they are synthesised live in your browser with the Web Audio API by the game code (engines, horns including the “Sindu horn” melody, rain, thunder, UI sounds, NPC sounds). No third-party recordings are used.

Music

The music player ships with silent placeholder tracks. Licensed or original tracks added later will be credited here (title, artist, licence, source).
