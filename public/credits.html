Sound & music credits

Engines and horns: the three-wheeler and bus engine sounds and the Air, Route, Twin Trumpet and City Beep horns were generated for this game with Higgsfield (Mirelo sound-effects model), then cut into seamless loops that blend and re-pitch with engine RPM.

Everything else is original and synthesised live in your browser with the Web Audio API: the “Sindu Melody” horn, tyre squeal, police siren, rain, thunder, crash, boost and UI sounds. No third-party recordings are used.

Music

Music is streamed from YouTube through the official embedded player. No music is bundled with the game.

Art

Vehicle models, city images, building textures and control art were generated with Higgsfield for this game.

Pedestrians, dogs and traffic police are animated: master stills (Higgsfield GPT Image) and motion clips (Kling via Higgsfield) were keyed, registered and quality-checked with Agent Sprite Forge (MIT licence, © 0x0funky), then packed into one animated sprite sheet.
